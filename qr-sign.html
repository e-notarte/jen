Connecting to deverajenschel22@gmail.com

Follow these 4 simple steps in Google Drive so guests can upload directly to your wedding album without needing permission requests:

1
Sign in to Google Drive with deverajenschel22@gmail.com and click + New > New Folder. Name it e.g. "Mark & Jenschel Wedding Photos".
2
Right-click the new folder and select Share > Share.
3
Under General access, change from Restricted to "Anyone with the link", and change the role to "Editor". (This lets wedding guests upload photos directly!).
4
Click "Copy link", paste it into the Destination Link box above, and click Print Sign!
